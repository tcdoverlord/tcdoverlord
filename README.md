## 🚀 Angel AI Harness

My current flagship project is **Angel AI Harness** — a local-first AI
engineering platform focused on building AI systems that remain understandable,
testable, recoverable, and under user control.

### Core Areas

**Evidence-Backed Reasoning**  
Reasoning workflows designed around evidence, verification, and traceability.

**Context Integrity**  
Protecting conversation and task context from unrelated or incorrect state.

**Persistent Local State**  
Local persistence designed around maintaining useful system and conversation
state.

**Controlled Execution**  
Tools and system actions remain explicit and controllable rather than hidden.

**Windows & Linux**  
Designed around practical local deployment rather than requiring a cloud-only
environment.

**Testing & Validation**  
Building verification into the development process instead of treating it as
an afterthought.
